TRACK 01 · HOW IT WORKS

A 40,000-word PDF in, one seamless audiobook out.

The splitter is the unsung hero — here's the journey every long document takes through the studio.

01

Paste anything

Articles, EPUB excerpts, lecture notes, screenplays. If it's text, the studio takes it — no length ceiling.

02

Split at natural pauses

The smart splitter reads punctuation and sentence rhythm, cutting into synthesis-sized chunks at real sentence boundaries.

03

Synthesize in parallel

Chunks render across background isolates — several neural voices working at once while your UI never drops a frame.

04

Stitch losslessly

Every chunk joins into a single gapless master — export one WAV or MP3 that sounds like it was recorded in one take.
