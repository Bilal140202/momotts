SIDE B · INSTALL

Three steps, ninety seconds.

Sideloading an indie APK is a one-time dance. After this, updates flow the same way.

Allow installs from your browser

Open Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps and grant your browser (Chrome, Firefox…) permission to install packages. This is Android's standard gate for anything outside the Play Store.

Download and verify

Grab the build that matches your device above. Careful people verify first: the official SHA-256 values are published in SHA256SUMS.txt and listed below — compare before installing.

Install, then meet Momo

Tap the APK, confirm, open MomoTTS. First stop: the Voice Hub — download a voice over Wi-Fi once, and from that moment the studio never needs a network again.
