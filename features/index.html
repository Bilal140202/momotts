T-01 · SMART TEXT SPLITTER

Long documents, treated like arrangements.

Neural voices shine on sentence-sized takes; feed a model a 9,000-character wall of text and quality collapses. MomoTTS refuses to let that happen. The splitter studies punctuation, sentence rhythm, and abbreviation traps (think "Dr.", "e.g.", decimals) and cuts your text at the pauses a human narrator would actually take.

Each chunk renders independently, then rejoins into one gapless master. You never see the seams — you just get a file that sounds like one continuous session.

  • No hard length ceiling — paste a full book chapter and walk away.
  • Abbreviation-aware splitting avoids mid-sentence guillotines.
  • Chunk queue is re-orderable and editable before synthesis.

T-02 · MULTI-VOICE DIALOGUE CASTING

Write a script like a chat log. Get an audio drama.

Prefix any line with a character name — MOMO:, NARRATOR:, GUEST: — and the casting engine assigns each speaker a distinct neural voice automatically. Change a character's voice once and every one of their lines re-renders.

It's the fastest path from "funny group-chat transcript" to "produced multi-cast episode" — podcast cold-opens, drama skits, language-learning dialogues, children's stories with a full cast.

  • Automatic per-character voice assignment with manual override.
  • Cast presets you can save and reuse across projects.
  • Per-line re-render — fix one flubbed line without re-doing the take.

T-03 · NEURAL VOICE HUB

A roster of voices that lives on your phone.

The hub ships with twelve-plus high-definition voices built on two proven engine families — Piper VITS for crisp, efficient narration and Kokoro 82M for expressive, emotive delivery. Every model is downloaded once, cached locally, and runs forever offline.

Storage anxiety is handled with the same care: a one-tap cache cleaner shows exactly what each voice occupies, so you keep the cast you use and evict the ones you don't.

  • 12+ bundled HD voices across both engine families.
  • Wi-Fi-friendly on-demand model downloads.
  • Transparent per-voice storage readout and cache cleaner.

T-04 · ZERO-FREEZE ISOLATE ARCHITECTURE

Synthesis in the basement, UI on the ground floor.

Neural inference is heavy work — most apps that try it shove the render onto the main thread and let the frame rate die. MomoTTS was built the correct way from day one: every synthesis job runs inside background worker isolates, communicating with the interface through lightweight message passing.

The result is boring in the best way — you can type the next script, scroll your library, or queue another export while the previous render is still hot. The UI stays at 60 FPS (120 on capable panels), always.

  • Background isolates for all inference — main thread never blocks.
  • Parallel chunk rendering for long documents.
  • Queue multiple projects; the studio meters the load.

T-05 · STUDIO AUDIO EXPORT

Masters that behave in a real NLE.

Exports land as lossless 16-bit 24 kHz WAV — the format podcast and video editors expect — or as MP3 when size matters more than bit-perfection. Files carry proper metadata, drop straight into Premiere, Audacity, CapCut or a DAW, and share to anything on your device via the standard share sheet.

Built-in playback gives full seeking, background listening, and per-project history, so yesterday's take is always one tap from tomorrow's revision.

  • WAV 16-bit / 24 kHz lossless masters.
  • MP3 exports tuned for voice at sensible bitrates.
  • Standard share-sheet integration — no lock-in, no walled garden.

T-06 · OFFLINE SANDBOX

Airplane mode is not a degraded state. It's the design.

There is no telemetry pipeline to disable and no account to "temporarily" require. Models, text, and audio live inside the app's private storage on your hardware. The only network call MomoTTS ever makes is the one you request: pulling a new voice model from the hub.

That architectural decision pays you back constantly — basement studios, flights, dead zones, exams, or simply the refusal to let a speech app phone home.

  • No analytics SDK, no ad framework, no crash-telemetry beacon.
  • No dangerous permissions requested — storage stays sandboxed.
  • Everything works offline except optional voice downloads.
